## Gaps and decision

- No real `keel-morning` run: `git ls-remote origin` and the fetches are run
  against a local bare origin, not in Actions with `actions/checkout`'s
  credentials. The workflow's shell is checked as text and parsed as bash
  (tests/workflows.test.mjs), not executed end to end.
- Until phase 76 records ticks durably, climb's and the robot's ticks reach
  no place `finalPlan` reads unless someone pushes the plan branch; then the
  brief waits for `readyBy` and says "not finished by" for every build item.
  That is the truthful brief, and the reader is the one function to adjust.
- The card is published by `keel morning --card --yes` from a machine with
  isocan; no workflow republishes it on a tick yet.
- The schedule is one daily run at 17:07 UTC (just after the default
  readyBy); the passes finishing (`workflow_run`) start it the rest of the
  time. Adoption's cost preview prices it as a daily cron plus event runs:
  from `tests/adopt.test.mjs`'s 2-weighted-minute fixture run, 60 weighted
  minutes a month for the schedule (30 runs), plus each observed event run
  scaled to 30 days. In practice about four short decide jobs a day (the
  daily run and up to three pass completions), roughly 120 billed minutes a
  month on a private repo. A readyBy at another time waits for the next pass
  or daily run; a readyBy after the next night starts, on a night whose
  builds never finish, gets no brief.
