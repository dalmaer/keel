## Limits and remaining walk

Reads are bounded. A workflow whose retained history exceeds the cap can remain incomplete, even when recent observations are available; stopping at an old creation date would miss old runs retried recently. Confirmed observed usage over a configured bound can still establish an overrun; incomplete history cannot establish that a project is within its bound. Jobs are attributed by completion within the window and use their whole runtime, with that policy stated.

Keel now names its existing `check.yml` for reuse. A clean checkout of the committed implementation and a fresh successful default-branch CI run are required for the post-merge reuse walk; it has not yet been performed.
