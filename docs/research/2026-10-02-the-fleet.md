parts of it does every project share, and which belong to just one? The

respect.

## What every one of them has (the core practice)

- **AGENTS.md as the working guide.** It covers commands, the map, how the
  work is run, the rules and untrusted content. `CLAUDE.md`, where it exists,
  is one line pointing at it (duo).
- **Phases that own their status.** Each phase is a file with front matter, and
  `docs/ROADMAP.md` is generated from them and checked in CI. The vocabulary is
  `planned → designed → partial → built → lived-in (+ superseded)`. The
  sections are **Done when** and **Deliberately open**; ritmo adds Scope,
  Acceptance, Proof and Next action. isocan's projects use
  `docs/projects/<p>/phases.md` with `**Status:**` lines, which is a different
  container for the same idea.
- **Lessons as shapes.** All four use the same three columns: shape, cost,
  guard. duo marks inherited rows *(ledger N)*, and ritmo distinguishes a
  planned guard from one that is implemented.
- **Verify before you push, then follow the push until it serves.** ledger has
  `watch:deploy`. duo's deploy fetches the live build stamp. ritmo's evidence
  files record the Pages run.
- **Research notes**, dated, under `docs/research/`.
- **Untrusted-content rules** for inbox, research and vendor material.

## What most of them have

- **Renovate.** Ledger runs one weekly grouped lane, never merged without a
  person. isocan runs four lanes: daily patch/minor that automerges on green,
  Monday lockfile, Monday majors for a person, and Node on its own. duo and
  ledger carry `renovate.json`; cajones does not.
- **Issues map to phases** (duo, ritmo): every phase that isn't built has an
  issue, the issue carries the conversation, and the phase carries the verdict.
- **`@claude` on GitHub** (duo `claude.yml`): it opens PRs and a person merges.
- **Evidence records** (ritmo, `docs/evidence/` plus a template). Built
  requires one, and the generator checks the link.
- **Skills under `.agents/skills/`**, with `.claude/` doorways.

## What the larger two have, and keel should carry

- **The conductor** (isocan `/conduct`, in `.claude/skills/conduct/`, with
  `status.sh`). It briefs a builder, verifies the proof itself, records the
  trajectory, and commits each phase to main. It was revised in **`7227f325`
  (2 Oct 2026)** after an audit of 32 subagent transcripts, and the revision
  makes four changes:
  - builders test by file;
  - the conductor runs the deep check once, detached;
  - briefs point to docs rather than paste them, under 600 words, and reports
    are capped at 600;
  - the conductor pulls first, marks new files with `git add -N`, and stages
    an explicit list.

  Keel's conduct skill is adapted from that commit, with the source pinned.
- **The night shift** (isocan `changelog.yml`, `grade.yml`, `persona.yml`,
  `loop.yml`; ledger's generated `agent-*.yml`). Its rules:
  - Measure before fixing.
  - Bots write PRs, never to main.
  - At most one open machine PR per queue, drained by the machine.
  - Merges run the suite, because `GITHUB_TOKEN` PRs never trigger CI.
  - Commit steps use `git status --porcelain` and rebase-retry pushes.
- **Evals.** isocan's are the reference: deterministic graders with a
  self-test that must fail on a bad fixture, golden tasks, skill lift, and
  personas whose goals are numbers with bounds. Its rule is to measure what
  is measurable before asking a model's opinion.
- **Stitch Loop triage** (ledger, isocan). Findings are files, and the ranking
  is ours. An agent proposes and a person decides. This is the model for
  `keel learn`.
- **A daily changelog** (isocan): a nightly draft PR that is merged as a draft
  after three days.

## Drift already visible between the copies

- Ritmo's generator validates evidence, dependencies and sections. duo's and
  ledger's validate less.
- Ledger once gated `lived-in` on two weeks of use and removed that rule on 18
  Sep. Ritmo says "no arbitrary waiting period". duo doesn't say.
- isocan kept two copies of conduct, and the untracked one was twelve days
  stale (keel lesson 1).
- Action versions vary across repos: `@v4`, `@v5` and `@v7`.

## What this means for keel

The core practice above becomes keel's first practices (phase 1):

- phases with evidence (ritmo's generator, the strictest);
- lessons;
- conduct (isocan `7227f325`);
- AGENTS.md blocks;
- CI check.

The "most" list becomes optional practices switched on per project. The
one-project list is exactly what `adopt` (phase 4) must classify as seeded or
leave untouched.
